# Current-build balance tables

Generated from active configuration with `node server/tools/gdd-balance.js --write`. No player data is changed.

Odds assume no buffs and available bag space. Species percentages are per successful catch; chest tier percentages are per chest drop. EV means long-run expected value, not a guaranteed payout.

Stake is the only risk lever: every table below is a function of rod level and Stake alone. A higher Stake costs more Bait and bites less often, and pays for it in rarer fish and better chests -- see the Stake ladder table.

Hourly figures assume continuous fishing, supplied bait, immediate fish cash-in, all chests opened and gems appraised. No stock shortages, regeneration, animation delays, Gold upgrades or bag pauses. Regular 250 ms ticks round cast durations upward and the 400 ms recast delay to 500 ms. Real timing can be slower.
